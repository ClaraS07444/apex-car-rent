## No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser's local storage and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

## Request form

The request form is a demonstration. Your entries are only processed in your browser to calculate the price preview and to check your input. They are not transmitted and not stored. They are lost when you close or reload the page.

## Google Maps

On the contact page you can load a map from Google Maps. The map is only loaded when you click "Load map". Only then is data transmitted to Google, in particular your IP address and information about your browser. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. A transfer to Google LLC in the USA is possible.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG, which you give by clicking. Your consent is not stored; the next time you visit the page, the map is switched off again. You can use the page fully without loading the map.

The "Plan route" link opens Google Maps in a new tab. Google's privacy policy applies there.

## Fonts

The Manrope font is stored on this website's server. No connection to Google Fonts or other font providers is made when the page loads.
